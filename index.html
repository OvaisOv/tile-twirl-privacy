Tile Twirl Privacy Policy

Tile Twirl: Jelly Swap Puzzle, by Osly Games. Last updated: November 25, 2026.

This page explains what information the Tile Twirl game uses and why. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

The short version

You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress is saved on your own device. The game shows ads, and the ad service collects some device information to do that.

Information stored on your device

Your level, stars, gems, boosters, settings and daily streak are saved on your phone or tablet. This information stays on your device. We do not send it to our own servers, because we do not run any.

Advertising

Tile Twirl shows ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, measure how they perform and stop fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Your IP address, which can show your general area such as country or city
  • Information about how you use the app and the ads you see or tap
  • Basic device and diagnostic information, such as device model, system version and crash data

This information is collected by Google and handled under Google's own policies. You can read them here: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • If you live in a region where the law asks for it, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice later from the Settings screen in the game.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your device settings under Privacy and then Ads.

Purchases

If you buy something in the game, the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details.

Children

Tile Twirl is made for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Keeping and deleting data

Because your progress lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game. For data held by Google's ad service, please use the choices described above or Google's own privacy tools.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy or about the game? Write to us at tiletwirlsupport@gmail.com.
